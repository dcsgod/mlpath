⏱ 3–4 months full-time · Resources: Gilbert Strang, Spivak, Cover & Thomas
```

  • Cholesky decomposition
  • Positive definite / semi-definite matrices

  • Tensor products, Kronecker products
  • Spectral theorem, Perron-Frobenius

  • Convexity, convex sets, convex functions
  • Lagrange multipliers, KKT conditions
  • Saddle points, local vs global minima
  • Line integrals, change of variables
  • Implicit function theorem
  • Manifolds, tangent spaces (for differential geometry)
Probability Theory
Probabilistic Foundations
  • Probability spaces, sigma-algebras, measure theory basics
  • Random variables — discrete and continuous
  • PDFs, CDFs, PMFs
  • Joint, marginal, conditional distributions
  • Expectation, variance, covariance
  • Moment generating functions, characteristic functions
  • Law of large numbers, Central Limit Theorem
  • Markov's inequality, Chebyshev, Hoeffding

  • Concentration inequalities

  • Channel capacity
  • Minimum description length (MDL)

  • Convex optimization (Boyd & Vandenberghe)
```
